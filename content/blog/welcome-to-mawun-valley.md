Welcome to [Mawun Valley Farm](/stay-contribute) — a permaculture farm nestled in the hills of South Lombok, Indonesia, just minutes from some of the most beautiful beaches in the world.

We're not a hotel. We're not a hostel. We're something different — a working farm that opens its doors to travelers seeking authentic experiences, meaningful connections, and a slower pace of life.

---

## Our Story

Mawun Valley Farm started as a dream: create a place where sustainable agriculture, community, and hospitality come together. Today, that dream is a reality — gardens, fruit trees, animals, and cozy cabins overlooking the valley.

**What we grow:**
- Vegetables (tomatoes, peppers, leafy greens, herbs)
- Fruit trees (cashew, papaya, banana, citrus, passion fruit)
- Flowers and ornamentals

**Who lives here:**
- Goats (Coconut, Spinach, Kangkung, and friends)
- Chickens providing fresh eggs daily
- Cats keeping the property pest-free
- A rotating community of guests from around the world

## What Makes Us Different

### Farm-to-Table Dining

At [Noni's Café](/nonis), meals are prepared with ingredients grown steps from your table. Our legendary cinnamon rolls, fresh salads, and Indonesian dishes showcase what's possible when food doesn't travel thousands of miles to reach your plate.

### Authentic Experiences

We offer more than accommodation — we offer [experiences](/stay-contribute):
- Learn traditional cooking techniques
- Participate in farm activities
- Explore permaculture principles in action
- Connect with local Sasak culture

### Community, Not Just Customers

Guests don't just stay here — they become part of something. Shared meals, Friday BBQs, morning yoga, evening conversations under the stars. Our [Gather program](/gather) creates the magic of genuine human connection.

### Perfect Location

Three minutes to [Mawun Beach](/blog/beach-hopping-south-lombok). Fifteen minutes to surf at Selong Belanak. Twenty minutes to sunset at Bukit Merese. All the adventure of South Lombok with a peaceful home base to return to.

## Our Values

**Sustainability:** We practice what we preach. Composting, water conservation, organic growing methods, minimal waste. The farm improves the land rather than depleting it.

**Community:** This isn't a transaction — it's a relationship. We remember names, share stories, and build connections that last beyond checkout.

**Authenticity:** No pretense, no tourist bubble. Real Indonesian hospitality from a family that loves welcoming guests.

**Slow Living:** In a world of rush and noise, we offer space to breathe. No pressure, no itinerary requirements. Just be.

## Plan Your Visit

South Lombok is accessible from Bali by fast boat (2.5 hours) or flight (25 minutes). From Lombok Airport, we're about 30 minutes south.

**Best time to visit:**
- Dry season (May-October): Ideal weather, best for beaches
- Wet season (November-April): Greener landscapes, afternoon rains, fewer tourists

**How long to stay:**
- Minimum: 2-3 nights to decompress and experience the rhythm
- Sweet spot: 1 week to explore the area properly
- Extended: Monthly rates available for longer stays
