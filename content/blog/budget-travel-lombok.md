## Accommodation: Where to Sleep Cheap

### Book Direct for Better Rates

Many guesthouses offer **10-20% discounts** when you book directly via WhatsApp instead of through booking platforms. Platforms take commission; owners prefer keeping that margin or passing savings to you.

**How to do it:**
1. Find a place on booking sites
2. Google their direct contact
3. Message: "Hi, I found you on [platform]. Do you have availability for [dates] at a better direct rate?"

### Stay Longer, Pay Less

Weekly and monthly rates can be **30-50% cheaper** per night. If you have flexibility, always ask:
- "What's your weekly rate?"
- "Do you offer monthly discounts?"

A place charging 200,000 IDR/night might offer 1,000,000/week (saving 400,000) or 3,000,000/month (50% off daily rate).

### Go Inland, Save Money

Beachfront commands a premium. [Staying at farm stays like Mawun Valley](/stay-contribute) often costs **half as much** as beach accommodation — and you get more character, better food, and a real community.

### Consider Work-Exchange Programs

Our [Belong program](/belong) offers accommodation and meals in exchange for contributing to farm projects. It's an incredible way to extend your trip, learn new skills, and become part of something meaningful.

### Budget Accommodation Tips

- **Negotiate:** Prices aren't always fixed, especially in low season
- **Check multiple rooms:** If shown an expensive option, ask "Do you have anything simpler?"
- **Avoid Airbnb:** Local guesthouses are usually cheaper and more authentic
- **Read recent reviews:** Conditions change; rely on current feedback

## Eating Cheap (and Eating Well)

The secret to budget travel in Indonesia: **local food is delicious and dirt cheap**. You don't need to sacrifice quality — you just need to eat where locals eat.

### Warungs: Your Best Friend

Warungs are small family-run eateries serving authentic Indonesian food. Typical meal: **15,000-30,000 IDR ($1-2)**. 

**How to find good ones:**
- Look for places with many local customers
- No English menu? Usually a good sign
- Busy at lunchtime = fresh food
- Plastic chairs and simple setup = cheap prices

### Best Value Dishes

**Nasi Campur (Mixed Rice)** — 20,000-35,000 IDR
Rice with multiple small dishes: protein, vegetables, tempeh, sambal. Filling, balanced, delicious. Best lunch deal in Indonesia.

**Nasi Goreng (Fried Rice)** — 15,000-25,000 IDR
Indonesia's national dish. Ask for "special" to add egg and chicken.

**Mie Goreng (Fried Noodles)** — 15,000-25,000 IDR
Same concept, noodle version. Sometimes comes with vegetables and protein.

**Gado-Gado** — 20,000-30,000 IDR
Vegetable salad with peanut sauce. Vegetarian, filling, and healthy.

**Sate (Satay)** — 15,000-25,000 IDR for 10 sticks
Grilled meat skewers with peanut sauce. Chicken is most common.

### Meals-Included Accommodation

[Staying at Mawun Valley Farm](/stay-contribute) includes meals from [Noni's kitchen](/nonis). Do the math — it often works out **cheaper than eating out**, and you'll eat better. Fresh ingredients from the farm, proper home cooking, and no decision fatigue.

### Market Shopping

If your accommodation has a kitchen, hit the local morning market (pasar) for:
- Fresh tropical fruit
- Eggs (cheap and excellent)
- Vegetables
- Fresh bread from local bakeries

A market breakfast costs almost nothing and connects you with local life.

### Drink Smart

- **Water:** Refill bottles at water stations (5,000 IDR) instead of buying plastic bottles (10,000+ IDR)
- **Coffee:** Warung kopi costs 5,000-10,000 IDR vs. 35,000+ at tourist cafes
- **Coconuts:** Fresh kelapa muda (young coconut) from beach vendors: 15,000-20,000 IDR

## Free & Cheap Activities

The best things in South Lombok cost nothing or almost nothing:

### Completely Free

**All beaches are public** — Mawun, Selong Belanak, Tanjung Aan. Small parking fees sometimes (5,000-10,000 IDR), but beaches themselves are free.

**Hiking and walking** — [Bukit Merese sunset viewpoint](/blog/beach-hopping-south-lombok), rice paddy walks, coastal trails along headlands. All free, all spectacular.

**Sunsets** — Endless and always different. Find a headland, bring a snack, watch the show.

**Village wandering** — Explore traditional Sasak villages on foot. Smile, wave, maybe get invited for tea.

**Stargazing** — Head to any dark hilltop after dinner. The Milky Way is visible on clear nights.

**Swimming** — Every beach, every day. This is why you came.

### Cheap Activities

| Activity | Cost | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| Surf lesson | 150,000-200,000 IDR | At Selong Belanak |
| Board rental | 50,000-100,000 IDR/day | |
| Motorbike rental | 50,000-75,000 IDR/day | Best transport value |
| Waterfall entry | 10,000-20,000 IDR | Marcopolo, Benang Stokel |
| Snorkel gear rental | 50,000 IDR/day | |

### Skip the Expensive Tours

Most "tours" charge premium prices for things you can easily do independently:
- Rent a motorbike (75,000 IDR) instead of booking a "beach tour" (300,000+ IDR)
- Visit waterfalls yourself instead of joining a "waterfall trip"
- The only tours worth paying for: things requiring boats or specific expertise

## Transport Savings

### Rent a Motorbike

At **50,000-75,000 IDR/day ($3-5)**, this is the cheapest and most liberating way to explore South Lombok. You can reach every beach, stop wherever you want, and travel on your own schedule.

**Money-saving tips:**
- Negotiate weekly rates (often 250,000-350,000 for 7 days)
- Fill up at official Pertamina stations, not roadside sellers
- Check the bike thoroughly before renting; take photos of any existing damage

### Share Rides

Not comfortable on motorbikes? Find other travelers heading the same direction and split taxi/driver costs. Hostel notice boards and traveler Facebook groups are good for this.

### Walk More

South Lombok is surprisingly walkable:
- Mawun to Tampah beach: 30 min walk along the headland
- Within Kuta town: everything is walking distance
- Rice field walks from the farm are beautiful

### Airport Transfer Tips

- **Avoid airport taxis:** Overpriced and aggressive
- **Pre-book pickup:** We offer airport transfers; many accommodations do
- **Use Grab/Gojek:** If available, much cheaper than taxis
- **Share with other travelers:** Split costs with people on your flight

## Money-Saving Mindset

### Slow Down

Rushing between destinations burns money on transport and doesn't let you experience anywhere properly. Stay **3-4 nights minimum** in each location. Weekly rates save money and let you discover places beyond the obvious.

### Travel Off-Peak

**Shoulder season (April, May, October, November):** Lower accommodation prices, fewer crowds, weather still good.

**Avoid:** Peak season (July-August, Christmas/New Year) when prices spike 30-50%.

### Carry Cash Wisely

- ATMs charge 25,000-50,000 IDR per withdrawal
- Withdraw larger amounts (2-3 million IDR) less frequently
- Keep emergency USD or Euros for bad ATM days
- Many local places are cash-only

### Say Yes to Local Life

The richest experiences often cost nothing:
- Accept tea invitations from villagers
- Watch traditional ceremonies if invited
- Chat with warungs owners about their lives
- Play volleyball with local kids on the beach
