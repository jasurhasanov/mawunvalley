## Why South Lombok for Groups?

### Space Without Crowds

Unlike Bali's packed beaches and overbooked restaurants, South Lombok offers:
- Beaches big enough for your whole group
- Restaurants that can seat everyone together
- Accommodation with room to spread out
- Activities without fighting other tour groups

### Variety for Everyone

Groups rarely have identical interests. South Lombok delivers:
- **Adventure:** Surfing, paragliding, cliff jumping
- **Relaxation:** Pristine beaches, yoga, spa treatments
- **Culture:** Traditional villages, cooking classes, local markets
- **Nature:** Waterfalls, rice fields, sunset viewpoints

No one has to compromise when there's something for everyone.

### Value for Money

Group travel here is remarkably affordable:
- Accommodation rates drop significantly per person
- Private transport becomes cost-effective when split
- Group cooking classes and activities offer per-head savings
- Eating family-style at warungs is cheap and fun

## Planning Your Group Trip

### Step 1: Establish the Basics

**Key questions:**
- How many people? (Affects accommodation options)
- How long? (5-7 days is sweet spot for groups)
- What's the budget range? (Per person per day)
- What's the group's fitness/activity level?
- Any dietary restrictions or accessibility needs?

### Step 2: Choose Accommodation Style

**Option A: Stay Together**

Rent a villa or take over accommodation like [Mawun Valley Farm](/stay) where your group has the run of the place.

*Pros:* Maximum bonding, shared spaces, group meals easy
*Cons:* Less privacy, need to coordinate schedules
*Best for:* Close friend groups, retreats, team building

**Option B: Nearby But Separate**

Book multiple rooms/villas in the same area, come together for activities.

*Pros:* Personal space, couples/families can have privacy
*Cons:* Coordination harder, less spontaneous connection
*Best for:* Family reunions with different generations, groups with varied sleep schedules

**At the farm**, we can accommodate both approaches — taking multiple cabins for a group while maintaining common gathering spaces.

### Step 3: Plan the Activity Mix

**The 60/40 rule:** Plan 60% of time with structure, leave 40% free.

**Group activities (do together):**
- One "big" adventure (paragliding, waterfall hike)
- One cultural experience ([cooking class](/gather))
- Regular shared meals (breakfast daily, dinner most nights)
- Sunset sessions (low-effort, high-reward)

**Free time (individual choice):**
- Beach days at their own pace
- Solo surf sessions for the keen surfers
- Rest/reading time
- Exploring town independently

### Step 4: Handle Logistics

**Transport:**
- Groups of 4-6: Rent motorbikes individually
- Groups of 7+: Hire a minivan with driver (much easier)
- Mix: Core group in vehicle, adventurous members on bikes

**Costs to split:**
- Transport and driver
- Group activity bookings
- Shared meals
- Accommodation (if renting together)

**Costs to keep individual:**
- Personal food/drinks outside group meals
- Individual activities
- Shopping and souvenirs
- Tips (unless pooled)

**Pro tip:** Use a shared expense app (Splitwise, Tricount) from day one. Avoids awkward money conversations at the end.
