# Things to Do Thursday in Lombok: Weekly Activities & Events Worth Planning Around

When you're traveling without a fixed schedule, days can blur together. Monday feels like Thursday, which feels like Saturday. That's part of the freedom, but it can also lead to drifting — watching days pass without making the most of them.

Here's a secret about South Lombok: Thursday has become a special day. The weekly rhythms of local life, combined with regular expat and traveler events, make Thursday one of the best days to be here. Whether you're staying a week or a month, building Thursday into your plans gives you something to look forward to and a structure to hang your week around.

## Why Thursday Matters in South Lombok

Every community develops its rhythms. Markets happen on certain days. Religious ceremonies follow the calendar. Social events claim particular nights.

In the Kuta Lombok area, Thursday has become the day for:

- **Sunset volleyball** at Mawun Valley Farm (the main event)
- **Great surf conditions** (often, due to tide patterns midweek)
- **Pre-Friday preparation** (relaxed vibe before the weekend rush)
- **Local market activity** in surrounding villages

For travelers, Thursday is the midweek reset — enough time to have explored, late enough to need social connection, early enough that you're not exhausted from the weekend.

## The Main Event: Sunset Volleyball at Mawun Valley Farm

Every Thursday at 4 PM, a group of travelers, expats, and locals gather at Mawun Valley Farm for casual volleyball games on a grass court. It's become one of the most consistent social events in South Lombok.

### What Makes It Special

**Consistency:** Unlike spontaneous beach meetups that may or may not happen, volleyball is guaranteed every Thursday. Plan around it.

**All-levels welcome:** You don't need to be good. The games are friendly, teams are mixed, and the focus is fun over competition.

**The setting:** You're playing on a working farm, surrounded by hills, as the sun drops toward the horizon. It's genuinely beautiful.

**Built-in socializing:** After games, people naturally gravitate to [Noni's Farm Café](/nonis) for drinks and dinner. The volleyball is the activity, but the connections are the point.

### The Details

- **When:** Every Thursday, 4:00 PM
- **Where:** Mawun Valley Farm (about 20 minutes from Kuta town)
- **Duration:** Approximately 1.5 hours
- **Cost:** IDR 100,000 per person
- **Includes:** Court access, volleyball, 1L water, one drink from the café

### What to Bring

- Comfortable athletic clothes
- Shoes with grip (grass can be slippery)
- Sunscreen
- Cash (IDR 100,000)
- Optional: change of clothes, small towel

### How to Join

Visit our [events page](/gather) to join the volleyball WhatsApp group. You'll get reminders, updates if conditions change, and a way to connect with other players.

Or just show up. Walk-ins are always welcome.

## Building Your Perfect Thursday

Volleyball starts at 4 PM, but the day has many more hours. Here's how to make the most of them:

### Morning: Active Start

**Option 1: Dawn Patrol Surf**
Hit the water early before the heat sets in. Thursday mornings often have good conditions at the main breaks:
- **Selong Belanak** — Mellow waves, great for longboards
- **Gerupuk** — Multiple breaks, more variety
- **Mawun** — Close to the farm if you want to minimize transit

Surf from 6-8 AM, grab breakfast in Kuta, and you've earned your afternoon.

**Option 2: Hill Hike**
If you're not surfing, explore on foot while it's cool:
- **Bukit Merese** — Short hike, incredible views
- **Mawun Valley exploration** — Come to the farm early, walk the surrounding hills

**Option 3: Beach Morning**
Sometimes simple is best. Pick a beach, swim, read, relax. You've got an active afternoon ahead.

### Midday: Rest and Refuel

**Avoid the hottest hours:**
- Work from a café if you're a digital nomad
- Nap if you're on vacation mode
- Have a leisurely lunch

**Food suggestions near the volleyball:**
- Bring snacks to the farm and have a late lunch there
- Eat in Kuta and drive out afterward
- Grab something light — heavy meals before activity aren't great

**Plan your afternoon:**
- Confirm you're going to volleyball in the WhatsApp group
- Check transportation (scooter, ride share, or ask if anyone is driving from your area)
- Prepare your volleyball bag

### Afternoon: Volleyball Time

**3:00 PM:** Head toward Mawun Valley Farm. The drive from Kuta takes about 20 minutes, but leave buffer time.

**3:30 PM:** Arrive at the farm. Have a drink at [Noni's Café](/nonis), meet early arrivals, stretch if you want.

**4:00 PM:** Games begin. Warm up, divide into teams, play.

**5:30 PM:** Games wind down as the sunset peaks. This is the magic hour — golden light, tired muscles, good vibes.

### Evening: Continue the Connection

**Post-volleyball options at the farm:**

**Dinner at Noni's:** The natural choice. Farm-to-table food, good drinks, conversation with people you just played with. Many volleyball regulars make this the standard Thursday dinner spot.

**Drinks and sunset:** Even if you don't stay for a full meal, hanging around for the sunset view and a cold drink extends the experience.

**Walk the farm:** The animals are active in the evening, the gardens are peaceful, and the light is beautiful for photos.

**[Stay overnight](/stay-contribute):** If you really want to make the most of it, book a cabin. Wake up Friday on the farm, have breakfast, and you're already positioned for [Friday BBQ night](/gather).

**Back to Kuta:**

If you're heading back to town, you've got options:
- Dinner at a restaurant in Kuta
- Night market exploration (ask locals for current schedules)
- Drinks at a beach bar
- Early night to recover from volleyball

## Alternative Thursday Activities

Can't make volleyball? Or want to fill more of the day? Here are other Thursday-appropriate activities:

### Surf Through Sunset

If volleyball isn't your thing but sunset activities are, a late afternoon surf session offers similar vibes. The light turns golden, the crowds thin, and there's something special about riding waves as the day ends.

**Best spots for afternoon/sunset surf:**
- Selong Belanak — West-facing, gorgeous sunset backdrop
- Tanjung Aan — Mellower conditions, less crowded late day
- Gerupuk — If you want to maximize wave count

### Market Exploration

Local markets in the villages around South Lombok operate on rotating schedules. Ask around to find out if any Thursday markets are happening near you. These aren't tourist markets — they're where locals buy produce, spices, and everyday goods. Fascinating for cultural insight.

### Spa and Wellness

If your week has been physical, Thursday afternoon might be recovery time. Several spas in Kuta offer massage and traditional treatments. Getting a massage before volleyball ensures you're loose; getting one after helps recovery.

### Photography Golden Hour

Photographers: Thursday is your day to scout and shoot. The afternoon light in South Lombok is incredible, and having a fixed event (volleyball) gives you a deadline that forces you out during golden hour.

**Great Thursday photo locations:**
- Bukit Merese — Rolling hills, ocean views
- Mawun Beach — Crescent bay, usually quieter
- The roads above Kuta — Dramatic elevated perspectives
- Mawun Valley Farm — Animals, gardens, farm life

### Cooking Class

Some cooking classes at the farm happen by arrangement. A morning cooking class followed by afternoon volleyball makes for a full Mawun Valley Thursday. Contact us through the [events page](/gather) to inquire.

## Planning Your Week Around Thursday

Thursday isn't just a day — it's an anchor that helps structure your entire week.

### Sample Week in South Lombok

**Monday:**
- Arrival or recovery day
- Explore Kuta, orient yourself
- Easy beach time at Tanjung Aan

**Tuesday:**
- Morning surf at Gerupuk (boat trip for multiple breaks)
- Afternoon work or rest
- Dinner in Kuta

**Wednesday:**
- Selong Belanak full day: surf, lunch, beach, sunset
- Or: Explore further (Ekas, Desert Point if conditions work)
- Rest up for Thursday

**Thursday:**
- Morning surf or beach
- Light lunch
- **4 PM: Volleyball at Mawun Valley Farm**
- Evening at the farm or Kuta

**Friday:**
- Morning activity of choice
- Afternoon: Head to Mawun Valley Farm
- **Evening: [BBQ Night at the farm](/gather)**

**Saturday:**
- Longer adventure: Rinjani area, Gili islands, or intensive surf
- Or rest day if you've been going hard

**Sunday:**
- Gentle start, beach time
- Prepare for another week (or departure)

Notice how Thursday volleyball and Friday BBQ create a two-day social anchor in the middle and end of the week. Everything else flexes around these fixed points.

### For Longer Stays

If you're in Lombok for weeks or months:

**Weekly rhythm:**
- Thursday volleyball becomes your standing social commitment
- Friday BBQ your weekly community dinner
- The WhatsApp group connects you between events
- Relationships build over repeated contact

**Progressive involvement:**
- Week 1: Show up, meet people
- Week 2: Exchange WhatsApps, make surf plans
- Week 3: Suggest weekday activities with new friends
- Week 4+: You have a social circle

This is how long-term travelers and expats build community. Regular events create repeated contact; repeated contact creates friendship.

## Practical Thursday Information

### Weather and Seasons

**Dry season (May-October):** Thursday afternoon usually features clear skies, reliable sunset views, and comfortable playing conditions.

**Wet season (November-April):** Rain is possible but not guaranteed. Volleyball happens rain or shine unless conditions are extreme. The grass court drains well.

**Hot days:** We start at 4 PM specifically to avoid the midday heat. By game time, temperatures are dropping and the setting sun reduces intensity.

### Getting to the Farm

**From Kuta Lombok:**
- ~20 minutes by scooter or car
- Head west toward Mawun Beach, follow signs to Mawun Valley Farm
- Google Maps has accurate directions

**Transportation options:**
- Rent a scooter (most common)
- Grab/Gojek (may be limited availability for return)
- Ask in the volleyball WhatsApp if anyone is driving from your area
- If [staying at the farm](/stay-contribute), you're already there

### What If You Miss It?

Volleyball happens weekly. If you can't make this Thursday, there's always next Thursday.

Other regular events at the farm:
- **Friday BBQ Night** — Weekly evening gathering with grilled food and community
- **Cooking classes** — By arrangement
- **Farm stays** — Available daily

Check the [events page](/gather) for the full calendar.

## Why Regular Events Matter

Here's the thing about travel: it's easy to be passive. To let beautiful places wash over you without engaging deeply. To have pleasant but forgettable experiences.

Regular events force engagement. When you show up to the same place, with the same people, week after week, something shifts. You stop being a tourist passing through and start being part of a community, however temporarily.

Thursday volleyball isn't just a game. It's:
- A deadline that gets you to a beautiful place during golden hour
- A reason to meet people you wouldn't otherwise encounter
- A structure that prevents aimless drifting
- A potential entry point to the expat and long-term traveler community

The game matters, but the community matters more.
