## 🚤 1. Jet Ski at Mawun Beach

**📍 3 minutes from Mawun Valley Farm**

Feel the spray of the ocean as you race across the crystal-clear waters of Mawun Beach. The calm, crescent-shaped bay is perfect for jet skiing — wide enough for speed runs, yet sheltered enough to feel safe.

### What to Expect

Mawun Beach's protected bay means calmer waters than open ocean spots, making it ideal for both beginners and experienced riders. The beach faces west, so afternoon sessions give you stunning views as the sun drops toward the horizon.

### Practical Details

- **Cost:** Around 200,000-300,000 IDR for 15-20 minutes
- **Availability:** Rentals available directly on the beach, typically 9am-5pm
- **Best time:** Late afternoon for calmer waters and better light
- **What to bring:** Waterproof phone case, sunscreen, change of clothes

### Pro Tip

Negotiate the price before getting on — rates are flexible, especially during quieter periods. Ask for a longer session deal if you're with a group.

## 🪂 2. Paragliding at Skylancing

**📍 6 minutes from Mawun Valley Farm**

For the ultimate bird's-eye view of South Lombok's stunning coastline, nothing beats **paragliding at Skylancing**. Launch from the hills above Lancing Beach and soar over turquoise waters, white sand beaches, and lush green valleys.

### The Experience

Tandem flights are available for complete beginners — you're strapped to an experienced pilot who handles everything while you enjoy the view. The flight typically lasts 10-15 minutes, depending on wind conditions, and you land softly on Lancing Beach below.

### What You'll See

From 200+ meters up, you'll spot:
- The entire South Lombok coastline
- Mawun Beach's perfect crescent
- Rice paddies and coconut groves
- On clear days, Mount Rinjani in the distance

### Practical Details

- **Cost:** 800,000-1,200,000 IDR for tandem flight with video
- **Requirements:** No experience needed; weight limit typically 90-100kg
- **Best conditions:** Morning flights (7-10am) when thermals are optimal
- **Booking:** We can arrange this for you — just ask at [the farm](/experience)

### Safety Note

Only fly with licensed operators. Skylancing has trained pilots and proper equipment. Avoid anyone offering significantly cheaper flights on the beach.

## 🏄 3. Surf the Legendary Waves

**📍 15-20 minutes from Mawun Valley Farm**

South Lombok is a **surfer's paradise**, with breaks for every skill level. The coastline offers everything from gentle beginner waves to challenging barrels that attract pros from around the world.

### Best Spots by Level

**Beginners: Selong Belanak**
- Gentle, rolling whitewash waves
- Sandy bottom (safe for falls)
- Board rentals and lessons available on the beach
- 15 minutes from the farm

**Intermediate: Gerupuk Bay**
- Multiple breaks accessible by boat
- Consistent waves year-round
- Boat taxi from Gerupuk village
- 25 minutes from the farm

**Advanced: Mawi Beach**
- Powerful, hollow waves
- Reef break (booties recommended)
- Less crowded than Kuta area
- 20 minutes from the farm

### Surf Season

- **Dry season (May-October):** Bigger, more consistent swells
- **Wet season (November-April):** Smaller waves, better for beginners

After a big surf day, come back to [Noni's Cafe](/nonis) for a proper meal — the fish curry is perfect for recovery.

For more detailed surf information, check our complete [Surf Guide to South Lombok](/blog/surf-guide-south-lombok).

## 🧗 4. Cliff Jumping at Tanjung Aan

**📍 20 minutes from Mawun Valley Farm**

Ready to take the plunge? The rocky outcrops at **Tanjung Aan** offer various cliff jumping spots, from beginner-friendly 3-meter drops to more daring 8-meter leaps into deep, clear water.

### The Location

Tanjung Aan is famous for its unique "pepper sand" beach, but the real adventure is at the rocky headland between the two bays. Local kids often jump here — follow their lead to find the safest spots.

### Jump Heights

- **3-4 meters:** Perfect starting point, deep water landing
- **5-6 meters:** Intermediate, requires commitment
- **8+ meters:** Advanced only, check depth carefully

### Safety First

- **Always check the water depth** before jumping — dive down first
- **Never jump alone** — have a buddy watching
- **Avoid after rain** — water can be murky, hiding obstacles
- **Watch the locals** — they know the safe spots

### Practical Details

- **Cost:** Free (just beach entrance, around 10,000 IDR)
- **Best time:** Mid-morning when the sun lights up the water
- **What to bring:** Reef shoes helpful for climbing rocks

## 🌊 5. Marcopolo Waterfall Trek

**📍 30 minutes from Mawun Valley Farm**

Escape the coastal heat with a jungle trek to **Marcopolo Waterfall** (also called Benang Stokel). The hike takes you through lush forest before revealing a stunning cascade surrounded by tropical greenery.

### The Trek

The waterfall is located in the foothills of Mount Rinjani, offering a completely different landscape from the coastal areas. The trail passes through traditional villages and dense jungle, with the sound of rushing water growing louder as you approach.

### What to Expect

- **Hike duration:** 20-30 minutes each way
- **Difficulty:** Easy to moderate (some slippery sections)
- **The waterfall:** 20+ meter cascade into a natural swimming pool
- **Water temperature:** Cool and refreshing — perfect after the humid hike

### Practical Details

- **Cost:** Small entrance fee (20,000-30,000 IDR)
- **Best time:** Morning for fewer crowds and better light
- **What to bring:** Water shoes, towel, dry clothes, drinking water
- **Guide:** Optional but helpful — locals at the entrance offer guiding

### Pro Tip

Combine the waterfall trip with a visit to traditional Sasak villages along the way. It makes for a full cultural and adventure day. Ask us about arranging a guided tour from [the farm](/experience).

## 🔐 6. Escape Rooms in Kuta

**📍 15 minutes from Mawun Valley Farm**

When you need a break from the sun (or it's a rainy afternoon), head to Kuta Lombok for some **brain-teasing fun**. The escape rooms offer themed adventures that'll challenge your problem-solving skills.

### Why It's Great

- **Air-conditioned** — perfect mid-day activity
- **Group bonding** — great for families or friend groups staying at the farm
- **Something different** — break up beach days with mental challenges

### Practical Details

- **Location:** Central Kuta, near the main restaurants
- **Duration:** 60 minutes per room
- **Group size:** 2-6 people typically
- **Cost:** Around 150,000-200,000 IDR per person
- **Booking:** Walk-in usually fine, but book ahead for evenings

### Combine With

After the escape room, explore Kuta's restaurant scene or catch sunset at nearby **Tanjung Aan** or **Bukit Merese** viewpoint.
