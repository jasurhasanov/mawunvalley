## The WiFi Reality: An Honest Assessment

Let's address the elephant in the room first. South Lombok is **not Canggu or Ubud** when it comes to internet infrastructure. But for most remote work, it's absolutely workable.

### Current Speeds (2026)

**Café WiFi in Kuta town:**
- Typical: 15-30 Mbps download
- Good spots: 30-50 Mbps
- Best we've seen: 80+ Mbps at newer establishments

**Accommodation WiFi:**
- Varies wildly — always test before committing to long stays
- Ask for speed test results before booking
- Budget places: Often 5-15 Mbps
- Mid-range: Usually 15-30 Mbps
- Premium: Can reach 50+ Mbps

**Mobile Data (Your Essential Backup):**
- Telkomsel: Best coverage in South Lombok, decent 4G
- XL Axiata: Good alternative
- Expect: 10-30 Mbps depending on location and congestion
- Data packages: 20-50GB for 100,000-200,000 IDR

### What This Means for Your Work

**Works well:**
- Email, messaging, project management tools
- Document editing and collaboration
- Standard video calls (with some buffer time)
- Web development, writing, design work
- Async communication

**Challenging:**
- Multiple simultaneous video calls
- Large file uploads/downloads (plan for off-peak hours)
- Real-time streaming or broadcasting
- High-frequency trading or latency-sensitive work

**Pro tips:**
- Schedule important video calls for early morning (6-9am) when networks are fastest
- Always have mobile data hotspot as backup
- Download large files overnight
- Use asynchronous communication tools when possible
- Consider a portable WiFi device with multiple SIM slots

## Visa Considerations

### Tourist Visa (VOA)

- **Duration:** 30 days on arrival
- **Extension:** One 30-day extension possible (total 60 days)
- **Cost:** $500k IDR (~$35) on arrival, similar for extension
- **Process:** Extension through immigration office in Mataram (1-2 hours from South Lombok)

### B211A Visa (Social/Cultural)

- **Duration:** 60 days initially
- **Extensions:** Up to 180 days total
- **Cost:** $300-400 through agents
- **Process:** Apply before arrival or convert from VOA
- **Best for:** Stays of 2-6 months

### Second Home Visa

- **Duration:** 5-10 years
- **Requirements:** Proof of funds ($130,000+), insurance, various documents
- **Cost:** Higher but long-term solution
- **Best for:** Committed long-term residents

### Digital Nomad Visa (Coming Soon?)

Indonesia has discussed a specific remote worker visa. Check current regulations before planning — this landscape changes.

**Important:** Working "illegally" on a tourist visa is technically against regulations. Many nomads do it, but understand the (small) risks.

## Practical Logistics

### Getting Here

**From Bali:**
- Fast boat: 2-2.5 hours to Bangsal, then 1.5 hours to South Lombok (cheapest)
- Flight: 25 minutes Bali to Lombok airport, then 30 minutes to Kuta (fastest)

**From Elsewhere:**
- Direct international flights to Lombok (LOP) from Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, Jakarta
- Domestic connections from major Indonesian cities

### Transportation

**Scooter rental:** 50,000-75,000 IDR/day, 500,000-750,000 IDR/month
- Essential for nomad life here
- International license or Indonesian license technically required
- Helmet always (it's the law and the smart choice)

**Without a scooter:** Possible but limiting
- Grab/Gojek available but limited
- Private drivers for day trips
- Walking works within Kuta town

### Health & Safety

**Medical facilities:**
- Basic clinics in Kuta
- Proper hospital in Mataram (1.5 hours)
- Serious issues: Medical evacuation to Bali or Singapore

**Insurance:** Essential. Get proper travel/health insurance with evacuation coverage.

**Safety:** South Lombok is generally very safe
- Petty theft exists — don't leave valuables unattended
- Traffic is the main risk — drive carefully
- Surf safety — respect the ocean, know your limits

### Banking & Money

**ATMs:** Available in Kuta, but can run out of cash on busy weekends
- Withdraw enough for several days
- Have backup card from different bank
- International transaction fees apply

**Cash vs. Card:**
- Most local businesses are cash-only
- Tourist establishments usually accept cards
- Always have cash backup

## Is South Lombok Right for You?

### ✅ Perfect if you:

- Can work asynchronously (don't need constant video calls)
- Value peace, nature, and space over nightlife and scene
- Are self-motivated and don't need coworking energy
- Want to significantly reduce living costs
- Enjoy surfing, beach life, and outdoor activities
- Seek genuine cultural experience, not tourist bubble
- Can handle occasional connectivity hiccups gracefully

### ❌ Maybe not if you:

- Need ultra-reliable, high-speed internet for constant video calls
- Thrive on bustling coworking scenes and nomad social events
- Prefer urban convenience and walkability
- Want extensive nightlife and entertainment options
- Need immediate access to international-standard healthcare
- Get anxious without backup plans and redundancy

### The Honest Truth

South Lombok is **not for everyone**, and that's exactly why it works for those it suits. If you need Canggu's infrastructure and social scene, you'll be frustrated here. If you're escaping exactly that, you'll love it.
