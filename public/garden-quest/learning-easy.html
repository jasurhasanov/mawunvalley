Easy Mode Learning Guide

Congratulations on completing The Garden Code! This guide contains all the answers plus fascinating bonus facts to deepen your botanical knowledge.

🏆 You explored all 10 checkpoints!

🌿 Checkpoints 1-3: Answers & Discoveries

1
Reception

Which fruit is known as the 'King of Tropical Fruits'?

✓ B. Mango

India produces nearly half of the world's mangoes — over 20 million tonnes annually!

There are over 1,000 different mango varieties worldwide, each with unique flavor profiles.

2
Herbal Remedies

Which herb is commonly used to help with digestion?

✓ B. Ginger

Ginger can survive up to 10 months underground before harvest — the longer it grows, the spicier!

Ancient sailors chewed ginger root to prevent seasickness — still recommended today.

3
Banana Garden

What mineral are bananas famously high in?

✓ C. Potassium

Bananas are slightly radioactive due to potassium — but you'd need 10 million at once for danger!

Banana plants can "walk" up to 40cm as new shoots grow away from the parent plant.

🌿 Checkpoints 4-6: Answers & Discoveries

4
Vegetable Garden

Which vegetable is technically a fruit?

✓ B. Tomato

Tomatoes were once believed poisonous in Europe because acidic tomatoes leached lead from plates!

A tomato has more genes than a human — about 31,760 compared to our 20,000-25,000.

5
Pineapple Garden

How long does it take for a pineapple to grow?

✓ C. 18-24 months

A pineapple is actually 100-200 flowers fused together — each "eye" was a separate flower!

In the 1700s, pineapples were so rare you could rent one just to display at parties.

6
Strawberry Fields

Where are strawberry seeds located?

✓ B. On the outside

An average strawberry has about 200 seeds on its surface — each can grow into a new plant!

Strawberries are the only fruit with seeds on the outside.

🌿 Checkpoints 7-10: Answers & Discoveries

7
Citrus Garden

What vitamin are citrus fruits famous for?

✓ C. Vitamin C

The discovery that citrus prevents scurvy saved millions of sailors' lives.

Citrus peels contain 10x more vitamin C than the juice!

8
Mixed Fruits Garden

When does the dragon fruit flower bloom?

✓ C. Night

Dragon fruit flowers bloom at midnight, last only one night, and smell like jasmine.

Some farms hire workers for "midnight pollination shifts"!

9
Jabuticaba Garden

Where does jabuticaba fruit grow on the tree?

✓ C. Directly on the trunk

Jabuticaba can fruit up to 6 times per year, making the trunk look covered in purple pearls!

The fruit ferments within 3-4 days — why it's rarely exported and remains Brazil's secret.

🏆
Flower Jungle (Final)

Which flower is used for cooking AND natural pest control?

✓ B. Marigold

Marigold roots release thiophene, killing soil nematodes for up to 3 years!

In Mexico, marigolds guide spirits home in Day of the Dead celebrations.
