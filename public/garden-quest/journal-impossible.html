Most commercial banana cultivars are sterile triploids (3n). What is their chromosome number?

  • A. 22
  • B. 33
  • C. 44
  • D. 66

🔬 PhD Insight

Haploid n=11. Triploid bananas (3n=33) are seedless because meiosis fails with odd chromosome sets.
