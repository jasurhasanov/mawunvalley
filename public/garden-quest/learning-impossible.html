🔬 Checkpoints 1-3: PhD-Level Insights

1
Reception

Mangiferin belongs to which class of compounds?

✓ B. Xanthones

Mangiferin (C₁₉H₁₈O₁₁) is a C-glucosylxanthone being studied for antidiabetic properties — it may inhibit α-glucosidase enzymes.

Unlike most compounds, mangiferin is concentrated in mango bark and leaves, not the fruit — traditional medicine knew this first.

2
Herbal Remedies

Curcumin's bioavailability is limited due to rapid:

✓ B. Hepatic glucuronidation

The liver conjugates curcumin with glucuronic acid, reducing systemic bioavailability to less than 1%.

Piperine from black pepper inhibits glucuronidation, increasing curcumin absorption by 2000%!

3
Banana Garden

Triploid banana chromosome number?

✓ B. 33

With n=11, triploid bananas (3n=33) cannot undergo normal meiosis — why commercial bananas are seedless.

This sterility makes all Cavendish bananas vulnerable to diseases like Panama TR4.

🔬 Checkpoints 4-6: PhD-Level Insights

4
Vegetable Garden

Tomato ripening is regulated by which phytohormone?

✓ C. Ethylene

Ethylene (C₂H₄) activates ripening genes through the RIN-MADS transcription factor.

The CRISPR "non-browning" tomato works by modifying ethylene sensitivity genes.

5
Pineapple Garden

What does CAM photosynthesis stand for?

✓ B. Crassulacean Acid Metabolism

CAM plants fix CO₂ at night into malic acid, releasing it during the day for the Calvin cycle.

Pineapple's CAM uses 80% less water than C3 crops — ideal for climate-adaptive agriculture.

6
Strawberry Fields

Octoploid strawberry has how many chromosome sets?

✓ C. 8

Fragaria × ananassa (8n=56) arose from hybridizing F. virginiana and F. chiloensis in 1750s France.

Higher ploidy often correlates with larger fruit size.

🔬 Checkpoints 7-10: PhD-Level Insights

7
Citrus Garden

Limonene is classified as a:

✓ B. Monoterpene

Limonene (C₁₀H₁₆) has two isoprene units. D-limonene = orange scent; L-limonene = pine scent.

D-limonene is being researched as a "green solvent" and anticancer agent.

8
Mixed Fruits Garden

Dragon fruit belongs to which plant family?

✓ B. Cactaceae

Hylocereus is an epiphytic cactus using aerial roots — doesn't parasitize host trees.

Dragon fruit's color comes from betalains (not anthocyanins) — stable across pH ranges.

9
Jabuticaba Garden

Which sugar is attached to jabuticaba's anthocyanins?

✓ B. Glucose

Cyanidin-3-O-glucoside is primary. Glucosylation increases water solubility and stability.

Jabuticaba skin has 310mg/100g anthocyanins — superior to blueberries!

Flower Jungle

Linalool acts on which neurotransmitter receptor?

✓ B. GABA-A

Linalool modulates GABA-A receptors similarly to benzodiazepines, enhancing chloride ion influx.

Inhaled linalool reduces anxiety without motor impairment — aromatherapy has real neurochemical basis.
