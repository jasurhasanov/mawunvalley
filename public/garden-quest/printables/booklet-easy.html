10 pts per question • 100 max

A4 double-sided booklet — ready for printer

🖨️ Print Instructions

  1. Download the PDF (6 pages = 3 A4 sheets)
  2. Print double-sided, flip on short edge
  3. Use A4 Landscape, Actual Size (100%)
  4. Stack the 3 printed sheets in order
  5. Fold in half — pages will be in correct order!
