🎮 How to Play

Display at Reception

2 double-sided A5 cards — print, cut in half!

🖨️ Print Instructions

  1. Download the PDF (2 A4 pages)
  2. Print double-sided, flip on short edge
  3. Cut each A4 in half vertically
  4. You now have 2 identical double-sided A5 cards!
  5. Laminate for durability — display at Reception!
