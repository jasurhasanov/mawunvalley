Hard Mode Learning Guide

You tackled the deeper questions! This guide reveals all answers plus advanced botanical knowledge that even scientists find fascinating.

🏆 Hard Mode Complete — 25 pts each

🌿 Checkpoints 1-3: Deep Knowledge

1
Reception

The mango originated from which region?

✓ C. South Asia

The earliest mango cultivation dates to 4000 BCE in India — one of humanity's oldest cultivated fruits.

Buddhist monks carried mangoes to Southeast Asia around 400 BCE, considering them sacred.

2
Herbal Remedies

What compound in ginger gives it anti-inflammatory properties?

✓ C. Gingerol

When ginger is dried or cooked, gingerol transforms into shogaol — twice as pungent!

Gingerol inhibits the same inflammatory pathways as ibuprofen, naturally.

3
Banana Garden

How many times does a banana plant fruit before dying?

✓ A. Once

After fruiting, the mother plant dies but produces "pups" — genetic clones from its base.

All Cavendish bananas are genetically identical clones — one giant organism worldwide.

🌿 Checkpoints 4-6: Deep Knowledge

4
Vegetable Garden

Garlic's smell comes from sulfur compounds released when:

✓ C. Cells are damaged

Garlic's smell is a defense mechanism — allicin is toxic to bacteria and pests.

Waiting 10 minutes after crushing maximizes allicin — more flavorful and medicinal.

5
Pineapple Garden

Pineapple's meat-tenderizing enzyme is called:

✓ B. Bromelain

Bromelain is so aggressive that pineapple workers can lose their fingerprints!

Beyond cooking, bromelain treats burns and is being studied for cancer treatment.

6
Strawberry Fields

Strawberries are members of which plant family?

✓ C. Rose family (Rosaceae)

Apples, pears, cherries, almonds, peaches, and roses are all strawberry cousins!

Modern garden strawberries were only created in 1750s France by crossing American species.

🌿 Checkpoints 7-10: Deep Knowledge

7
Citrus Garden

The orange was first cultivated in which region?

✓ B. Southeast Asia

The word "orange" traveled Sanskrit → Persian → Spanish → English. The color was named after the fruit!

Oranges are a hybrid — ancient farmers crossed pomelo with mandarin thousands of years ago.

8
Mixed Fruits Garden

What does 'rambutan' mean in Indonesian/Malay?

✓ C. Hairy

"Rambut" means "hair" — the fruit's name literally describes its fuzzy appearance!

The "hairs" change color as it ripens: green → yellow → red.

9
Jabuticaba Garden

Jabuticaba is native to which country?

✓ C. Brazil

The fruit spoils within 3-4 days — why it's rarely found outside Brazil.

In Brazil, jabuticaba is fermented into wine and liqueur.

🏆
Flower Jungle

What compound in lavender is responsible for its calming effects?

✓ B. Linalool

Linalool is found in 200+ plants including mint, cinnamon, and citrus.

Studies show inhaled linalool reduces anxiety as effectively as Valium, without sedation.
