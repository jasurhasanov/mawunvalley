How to Play

Garden Quest Adventure

🗺️ Your Journey

  1. 1 Pick up your Quest Journal at Reception and choose your difficulty level
  2. 2 Follow the map to find 10 checkpoints throughout the garden
  3. 3 At each checkpoint, answer the botanical challenge in your journal
  4. 4 Return to Reception to check your answers and claim your reward!
